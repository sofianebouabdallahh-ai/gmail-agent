# PDF attachments

The text arrives page by page, each page starting with `[page N]`.

- **Invoices and receipts**: find the issuer, invoice/receipt number, issue date, due
  date, total (with tax if shown), currency, and payment method or bank details.
  The total is usually on the last page; do not mistake a subtotal for it.
- **Insurance documents and ID cards**: policy number, insured names, vehicle or
  property, coverage period (start and end dates), insurer contact.
- **Tickets and boarding passes**: passenger names, booking reference (PNR), flight or
  train number, origin → destination, departure date and time, seat, gate if present.
- **Contracts and letters**: parties, what is being agreed or asked, amounts, deadlines,
  and any signature or reply required.

A page that comes back empty is usually a scanned image: say "page N appears to be a
scan, text not available" rather than guessing its content.
